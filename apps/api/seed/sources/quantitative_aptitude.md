## Judgement calls

- SSC links number_system, arithmetic, geometry, mensuration and trigonometry as whole units. From algebra it links only algebraic_identities and linear_equations (the "graphs of linear equations" item); from data interpretation, only the data_interpretation chapter (histogram, frequency polygon, bar diagram, pie chart; tables and line graphs are standard SSC DI). Elementary surds are under number_system/powers_and_roots. The section is split across several links and SSC gives no per-part shares, so no weightage is set.
- CSAT is officially only "basic numeracy" and "data interpretation". Its links (number system, arithmetic, Class X algebra, geometry, mensuration, all of data interpretation including data sufficiency and basic statistics, and counting and probability) follow what recent CSAT papers actually ask. Trigonometry is left out because CSAT does not test it.
- RPSC School Lecturer Paper I (rpsc_1g_paper_1) links exactly its line items. No share is stated. The links are:
  - real numbers and decimal expansions: number_system/real_numbers, a chapter added for this paper;
  - laws of exponents: powers_and_roots/indices;
  - zeroes of a polynomial and their relationship with coefficients: polynomials_and_quadratics/polynomials;
  - division algorithm for polynomials: polynomials_and_quadratics/division_algorithm, a topic added for this paper;
  - pair of linear equations: linear_equations/pair_of_equations;
  - collection and presentation of data, and mean, median and mode of ungrouped and grouped data: basic_statistics, now split into mean, median and mode topics;
  - graphical representation of data: the bar-diagram and histogram/frequency-polygon topics;
  - surface area and volume of the cuboid, cube, cylinder, cone and sphere, and combination and conversion of solids: the matching mensuration/solids topics. Prism and pyramid are not included.
- Counting and probability (permutations, combinations, probability) and progressions are in the tree for CSAT. SSC CGL Tier 1 does not list them.
- Hindi names follow NCERT Hindi-medium mathematics books (महत्तम समापवर्तक, करणी, सर्वसमिका, समान्तर श्रेढ़ी, उन्नयन कोण). The common coaching usage "एलिगेशन" is kept for alligation.
