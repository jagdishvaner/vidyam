## Judgement calls

- Senior Teacher 2025 (2g): the current line items are nature and scope; holistic development; learning (behavioural, cognitive, social-cognitive, constructivist); personality, adjustment and mental health; intelligence, creativity and emotional intelligence; motivation; individual differences and inclusive education; and 21st century skills. Older 2024 items that teachers still teach and that past papers test were kept as topics: transfer of learning, defence mechanisms and maladjustment, achievement motivation, gifted, slow-learner and delinquent children, and self-concept, attitudes, interests, habits, aptitude and social skills. "Schools of psychology and psychologists' contributions" comes from older syllabus wording and is linked through the foundations unit.
- School Lecturer 2025 (1g): Paper I does include educational psychology. It is section (v), "Educational Psychology, Educational Management, Educational Scenario in Rajasthan, NEP 2020, RTE Act 2009". The whole section lives in this tree (units educational_management and education_system_and_policy), and 1g links only the chapters its line items name.
- The teaching_learning unit (classroom communication, models of teaching, TLM and cooperative learning, ICT) is School Lecturer Paper II Part IV. It is shared by every 1g subject paper, but catalog.json does not list educational_psychology for the rpsc_1g_p2_* papers, so nothing links it. Only its 21st-century-skills chapter is linked, from 2g.
- No weightage is given. The RPSC PDFs do not state section shares. Third-party sites quote 20 of 100 questions (40 marks) for 2g, but that figure is not official.
- Hindi names follow RPSC and RBSE usage (अधिगम, अभिप्रेरणा, समायोजन, सृजनात्मकता, संवेगात्मक बुद्धि, शैक्षिक प्रबन्धन).
