- **SSC CGL Tier-I English Comprehension.** ssc.gov.in serves notices through a script-driven app, so the 2025 notification PDF could not be fetched. The official Tier-I line is generic: "ability to understand correct English, basic comprehension and writing ability". Coverage therefore uses the question types of the CGL English syllabus as tested in Tier-I papers:
  - spot the error, fill in the blanks, synonyms and antonyms, spellings, idioms and phrases, one-word substitution
  - improvement of sentences, active/passive voice, direct/indirect narration
  - shuffling of sentence parts and of sentences, cloze passage, comprehension passage

## Judgement calls

- **REET.**
  - Level 1 and Level 2 list identical English content.
  - All three REET papers link the union of Language I and Language II.
  - BSER has not republished separate language syllabi since the 2015 PDFs. The 2021, 2022 and 2024 cycles reused them, but this is an assumption to recheck at the next REET notice.
- **RPSC School Lecturer erratum.** The syllabus prints "Shall I Compare Thee to a Summer's Day (Sonnet 130)". That poem is Sonnet 18; Sonnet 130 is "My mistress' eyes…". The tree follows the title (`shakespeare_sonnet_18`). RPSC should be asked if this matters.
- **Same play, two nodes.** Chekhov's "A Marriage Proposal" (RPSC 2nd Grade) and "The Proposal" (First Flight) are the same play. They are kept as separate nodes because the textbook version is an abridged lesson, and each has the other's title as an alias.
- **Prose vs fiction.** "The Last Lesson" and "The Enemy" are listed as prose by RPSC 2nd Grade. They are short stories, so they sit under "Novels and Short Stories".
- **Teaching models and ICT.** School Lecturer Part IV is generic pedagogy common to all subjects. It sits in the English pedagogy unit so that `rpsc_1g_p2_english` can link it; the Hindi tree has its own copy.
- **CBSE scope.** CBSE Class 10 coverage follows English Language and Literature (184), which nearly all schools take. English Communicative (101) is not modelled.
  - The Words and Expressions-II workbook is not a separate node; its grammar is covered by the grammar chapters.
- **RBSE vs CBSE.** RBSE lists no modals in its grammar section. It adds Report/Essay/Story/Blog writing, so it links the whole composition chapter.
- **Graduate and PG material** sits in its own units: literary terms, literary history, varieties of language, prescribed poetry and prescribed prose/fiction/drama.
- **Weightage is omitted throughout.** Section marks (CBSE/RBSE 20/20/40, SSC 25 questions, REET 30 questions) do not map one-to-one onto single nodes.
