## Design and judgement calls

- **One tree for all levels.** School units come first, then graduation units, then pedagogy.
  - School units are topic areas: number system, arithmetic, algebra, sets/relations/functions, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, calculus, vectors & 3D, LPP, statistics & probability.
  - Graduation units: abstract algebra, linear algebra, real analysis + metric spaces, complex analysis, advanced calculus, ODE/PDE, vector calculus, solid geometry, statics & dynamics, integral transforms & special functions, differential geometry & tensors, numerical analysis + difference equations, optimisation.
  - The `pedagogy` unit covers REET "pedagogical issues", RPSC 2nd Grade Part III and RPSC 1st Grade Part IV.
- **RPSC's level labels are not used as units.** RPSC 1st Grade calls real analysis, linear algebra and numerical analysis "post-graduation". RPSC 2nd Grade puts real analysis and numerical analysis in "graduation". Both exams therefore link the same graduation units.
- **Board exams link only their own class.** CBSE and RBSE class 10 and class 12 boards link only that class's content, at topic level where the 2026-27 rationalised syllabus drops part of an NCERT chapter. Examples of dropped parts: elementary transformations, properties of determinants, MVT, tangents & normals, area between curves, formation of DEs, triple products, planes, random variables. CBSE 10 and RBSE 10 have identical coverage.
- **Topics that only some exams cover.** Some school topics are no longer linked by any board but are still reached through RPSC's whole-chapter links: areas of similar triangles, conversion of solids, areas of combinations of figures, and exponential/logarithmic series (1st Grade only).
- **Content no exam links.** These nodes are in the tree only to complete the class 1–9 curriculum:
  - decimals, integers and rational numbers (the current REET lists don't name them);
  - multiplication/division and equivalence of fractions;
  - linear equation in two variables (class 9) and the cross-multiplication method;
  - polygons, symmetry and visualising solids;
  - Euclid's geometry and practical geometry;
  - the problem-solving method (not named by RPSC).

  If the coaching centre wants REET Level 2 to include integers and rationals, as older REET syllabi did, add those chapters to `reet_level_2_science_math`.

- **JEE: trigonometric equations are linked.** The NTA text only says "trigonometrical identities and functions", but trigonometric equations still appear in JEE Main papers. JEE does not link heights & distances, mean value theorems, tangents & normals, triple products, planes or the binomial distribution, which were removed in 2024.
- **REET Level 2: partnership.** REET Level 2's ratio-and-proportion line ("समानुपाती भाग") was linked to the whole ratio chapter, including partnership (साझा). The partnership item comes from earlier REET Hindi syllabi and could not be re-verified in an official 2025/26 PDF.
- **Weightage omitted.** No official document gives a per-node share for these papers. CBSE and RBSE give unit marks inside the maths paper, but these don't map one-to-one to the topic-area units here. They belong in a paper pattern (blueprint), not in coverage:
  - CBSE X (80): Number Systems 6, Algebra 20, Coordinate Geometry 6, Geometry 15, Trigonometry 12, Mensuration 10, Statistics & Probability 11.
  - CBSE XII (80): Relations & Functions 8, Algebra 10, Calculus 35, Vectors & 3D 14, LPP 5, Probability 8.
  - RBSE XII uses the same unit marks as CBSE XII.
