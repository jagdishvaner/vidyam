## Judgement calls

- **One tree for both papers.** The tree is the union of the Senior Teacher and School Lecturer lists, organised
  as geography, history up to the 18th century, the freedom struggle and integration, art and culture, polity and
  administration, economy and schemes, and current affairs. The Senior Teacher list says "History upto 18th
  Century" and the Lecturer list says "8th to 18th Century". Both are covered by the `history` unit, which also
  holds sources of history, janapadas and the treaties of 1818. The treaties of 1818 sit in the 18th-century
  chapter as the end of that period.
- **Coverage follows the 2025 syllabi.**
  - Senior Teacher links these geography chapters: location, physiography, climate, drainage, water
    resources and irrigation, vegetation, agriculture, livestock and dairy, population, tribes, industries and
    tourism. It does not link soils, minerals, energy or transport, which its bullets do not list. It also links
    the whole history, freedom-struggle, art and culture, and polity units, the welfare-schemes chapter (its
    "New Schemes and Initiatives") and current affairs.
  - School Lecturer links the whole geography unit. Its list includes soil, minerals, energy, transport and
    trade, and tribes fall under "demographic characteristics". It also links history, the freedom struggle,
    the culture chapters it names, all of economy and schemes ("flagship schemes, skill development and
    startup initiatives, social and economic indicators") and Rajasthan current affairs. Handicrafts, Leading
    Personalities and the polity unit are not in the Lecturer list, so they are not linked for it.
- **Irrigation and the Indira Gandhi Canal** are linked for Senior Teacher even though its bullets say only
  "Drainage" and "Agriculture". Questions on them appear regularly, and they are normally taught under those
  heads.
- **No weightage.** Neither 2025 RPSC Paper I syllabus gives a marks split. They give only the totals: 200 marks
  and 100 questions for Senior Teacher, and 150 marks and 75 questions for School Lecturer. Coaching sites quote
  80/20/60/40 for Senior Teacher, but that split is not in the official document, so it was not used.
- **Educational Scenario in Rajasthan** (SCERT, BSER, DIET, RSOS and so on) is in School Lecturer Paper I. It is
  deliberately left out here because the `educational_psychology` tree already covers it (`education_system_and_policy`).
- **Current affairs and schemes:** time-bound items, such as particular schemes or awardees, are left to tagging.
  The nodes are stable categories ("Health and Hygiene Schemes", "State Awards and Honours"), not named schemes
  that change with each government.
- **Hindi names** follow RBSE and RPSC usage, for example भौतिक प्रदेश, अपवाह तंत्र, प्रजामंडल, लोक देवता एवं लोक देवियाँ,
  संभागीय आयुक्त and राजस्थान राज्य विधिक सेवा प्राधिकरण.
