# Environmental Studies: sources and judgement calls

Tree: `syllabi/environmental_studies.json` (11 units, 35 chapters, 149 topics). Coverage: `coverage/environmental_studies.json`.

## Documents used

- **REET 2024 syllabus, Level 1, Paper I Section (v) Environmental Studies** (30 Q), BSER Ajmer, Hindi and English. The official PDF is mirrored at <https://static.collegedekho.com/media/uploads/2025/02/12/reet-level-1-syllabus-2025-pdf-in-hindi.pdf>, page 17 in Hindi and page 18 in English.
  - It lists: family; clothes and habitats; profession; our culture and civilisation; general knowledge of Rajasthan; transport and communication; personal hygiene; living beings; water; the earth and space; mountaineering.
  - It also lists the EVS concept and scope, and environmental pedagogy.
- **NCERT EVS books.** NCERT has no separate EVS book for Classes 1–2.
  - _Looking Around_, Classes 3–5. Its six NCF-2005 themes (family and friends, food, shelter, water, travel, things we make and do) shape the units.
  - New _Our Wondrous World_, Classes 3–5. Chapter titles were checked on secondary sites (vedantu, oswaal, odinnclasses) and added as aliases.

## Judgement calls

- **Units.** The units follow the NCERT themes, with REET's Rajasthan items kept together under `our_culture_rajasthan`: fairs, festivals, folk deities, heritage, painting, proverbs, personalities and general knowledge of Rajasthan.
- **Extra nodes.** A few nodes cover NCERT and older-REET content that REET 2024 does not list: public places and institutions, work and play, materials and energy, air and weather. REET does not link them.
- **Overlap with social science.** The `rajasthan_gk` chapter is primary-level. Deeper Rajasthan history and geography lives in `social_science` (and in `rajasthan_gk`, owned by another subject file).

## Coverage notes

- **REET Level 1** links the 2024 list: the whole units that match and the specific chapters for the rest.
- Under food, only the balanced-diet topic is linked, because REET puts it under personal hygiene.
- No content/pedagogy split is published, so there is no weightage.

## Uncertain

- No REET notification after 2024 was found. Some coaching sites still show the older (2021/22) EVS list, which had matter and energy, public places, and basic needs; those nodes exist but are not linked.
