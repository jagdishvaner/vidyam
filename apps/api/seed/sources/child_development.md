## Judgement calls

- Levels 1 and 2 have the same CDP syllabus, with two differences. Level 2 names the learning theories (behaviourism, Gestalt, cognitivism, constructivism) and lists constructivist approaches, experiential learning, concept mapping, investigatory approach and problem solving under "how children learn". Level 1 says only "theories of learning" and "reflection, imagination and argument". These approaches also fall under Level 1's "NCF 2005 strategies and methods", so all three REET papers link the whole subject with weightage 0.2 (30/150).
- Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Erikson and Freud are not named in the syllabus. REET tests them under development dimensions and learning theories, so they are a chapter (development/development_theories).
- The Hindi names follow the REET Hindi syllabus text. The syllabus says "समग्र एवं सतत् मूल्यांकन" and "सीखने के प्रतिफल"; the more common "सतत एवं व्यापक मूल्यांकन" is used for the CCE node, with the REET phrase as an alias.
- "Contemporary child welfare educational schemes of Rajasthan" is grouped by kind of scheme (nutrition, girls' incentives, scholarships, free facilities) instead of by scheme name, because names change with each government. The aliases name the current schemes.
- RTE, NEP 2020, inclusive education and learning theories also appear in the educational_psychology tree. That subject serves the RPSC papers, and catalog.json gives each exam its own subject.
